# Microsoft — Company Profile, Eligibility and Selection Process

Source: Representative sample compiled for the ResolvAI prototype. Figures are illustrative
of a typical recent cycle and are not an official Microsoft notification. Always confirm
against the placement cell's circular for the current drive.

## About the Company
Microsoft is an American multinational producing software, cloud services (Azure), devices
and productivity tools. Campus freshers are hired as **Software Engineer (SWE)** and as
**SWE Interns**, with Azure, Office and Windows among the larger teams.

## Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum **7.0 CGPA** in the current degree is typical for campus shortlisting.
- **No active backlogs** at the time of the drive.
- Open primarily to CSE, IT and ECE branches.
- Consistent academic record across 10th, 12th and the degree is preferred.

## Number of Rounds
Microsoft conducts **4 rounds**:
1. **Online assessment** — two to three coding problems on Codility or a similar platform.
2. **Technical interview 1** — data structures and algorithms with optimal complexity.
3. **Technical interview 2** — algorithms plus operating systems and concurrency, and
   system design for senior profiles.
4. **AA round (As Appropriate)** — a senior manager assesses problem solving, culture fit
   and growth mindset. This round makes the final call.

## Cutoff
- Online assessment: at least two problems solved fully.
- **No fixed percentage cutoff** beyond the CGPA shortlist; coding performance decides.

## CTC and Role
- **Software Engineer:** approximately Rs. 45 to 51 LPA total compensation, comprising base
  salary, a joining bonus and stock awards.
- **SWE Intern:** stipend of approximately Rs. 1.0 to 1.5 lakh per month.
