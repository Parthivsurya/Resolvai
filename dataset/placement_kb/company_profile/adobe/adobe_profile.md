# Adobe — Company Profile, Eligibility and Selection Process

Source: Representative sample compiled for the ResolvAI prototype. Figures are illustrative
of a typical recent cycle and are not an official Adobe notification. Always confirm against
the placement cell's circular for the current drive.

## About the Company
Adobe is an American multinational known for creative, document and digital-experience
software including Photoshop, Acrobat and Experience Cloud. Campus freshers are hired as
**Member of Technical Staff (MTS-1)** and as **Product Interns**.

## Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum **7.0 CGPA** in the current degree is typical for campus shortlisting.
- **No active backlogs** at the time of the drive.
- Open primarily to CSE, IT and ECE branches.
- Strength in C++ and systems fundamentals is weighted heavily.

## Number of Rounds
Adobe conducts **4 rounds**:
1. **Online assessment** — aptitude and output-prediction questions plus two coding problems.
2. **Technical interview 1** — data structures, algorithms and C++ fundamentals.
3. **Technical interview 2** — object-oriented design, operating systems, and applied
   problems such as a PDF annotation or rendering pipeline.
4. **Hiring manager and HR round** — project depth, team fit and behavioural questions.

## Cutoff
- Online assessment: both coding problems solved, with the aptitude section cleared.
- **No fixed percentage cutoff** beyond the CGPA shortlist.

## CTC and Role
- **MTS-1:** approximately Rs. 28 to 34 LPA total compensation, comprising base salary,
  a joining bonus and stock units.
- **Product Intern:** stipend of approximately Rs. 80,000 to 1.0 lakh per month.
