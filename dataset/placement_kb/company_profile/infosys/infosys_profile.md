# Infosys — Company Profile, Eligibility and Selection Process

Source: Representative sample compiled for the ResolvAI prototype. Figures are illustrative
of a typical recent cycle and are not an official Infosys notification. Always confirm
against the placement cell's circular for the current drive.

## About the Company
Infosys is an Indian multinational IT and consulting company. Freshers are hired into three
tracks: **System Engineer (SE)**, **Digital Specialist Engineer (DSE)** and **Specialist
Programmer (SP)**, in increasing order of coding difficulty and compensation.

## Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum **60% or 6.0 CGPA** aggregate across 10th, 12th and the current degree.
- **No active backlogs** at the time of the drive.
- Gap in academics of **not more than 2 years**.
- Open to all engineering branches plus MCA and M.Sc (CS / IT).
- Candidates must not have appeared for an Infosys drive in the preceding 9 months.

## Number of Rounds
Infosys conducts **3 rounds**:
1. **Online assessment** — Reasoning Ability, Mathematical Ability, Verbal Ability, and a
   Pseudocode section. The SP track adds an advanced coding round of 2 to 3 problems.
2. **Technical interview** — 30 to 40 minutes on DBMS, OOP, data structures, SQL queries and
   project discussion.
3. **HR interview** — behavioural questions, relocation, and the service agreement.

## Cutoff
- SE track: clear all sections of the online assessment with roughly a **65% aggregate**.
- DSE track: higher assessment score plus performance in the technical round.
- SP track: a strong score on the advanced coding round is the deciding factor.

## CTC and Role
- **System Engineer:** approximately Rs. 3.6 LPA.
- **Digital Specialist Engineer:** approximately Rs. 6.25 LPA.
- **Specialist Programmer:** approximately Rs. 9.5 LPA.
- Training at the Mysuru campus is typical before project allocation.
