# Amazon — Company Profile, Eligibility and Selection Process

Source: Representative sample compiled for the ResolvAI prototype. Figures are illustrative
of a typical recent cycle and are not an official Amazon notification. Always confirm
against the placement cell's circular for the current drive.

## About the Company
Amazon is an American multinational in e-commerce, cloud computing (AWS), digital streaming
and artificial intelligence. Campus freshers are hired as **SDE-1** (Software Development
Engineer) and as **SDE Interns** across AWS, Retail and Operations Technology.

## Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum **6.0 CGPA** or 60% in the current degree. Amazon weighs the coding rounds far
  more heavily than academic percentage.
- **No active backlogs** at the time of the drive.
- Open primarily to CSE, IT, ECE and allied circuit branches.
- No strict restriction on academic gap, provided it is justified.
- Both B.Tech and M.Tech candidates are eligible.

## Number of Rounds
Amazon conducts **4 to 5 rounds**:
1. **Online assessment** — two coding problems (medium to hard) plus work-style and work
   simulation sections. Roughly 120 to 180 minutes.
2. **Technical interview 1** — one or two DSA problems with optimal complexity expected.
3. **Technical interview 2** — DSA plus low-level design, such as designing an LRU cache or
   a parking lot.
4. **Technical interview 3 / Bar Raiser** — a senior interviewer from another team assesses
   depth and the Leadership Principles. This round can independently reject a candidate.
5. **Hiring manager round** — team fit, project depth and behavioural questions.

Every interview round includes **Leadership Principle** behavioural questions alongside the
technical content. Prepare STAR-format stories.

## Cutoff
- Online assessment: generally both coding problems solved with all test cases passing.
- There is **no fixed percentage cutoff**; the coding rounds are the filter.

## CTC and Role
- **SDE-1:** approximately Rs. 44 to 48 LPA total compensation, comprising base salary,
  a joining bonus and restricted stock units vesting over four years.
- **SDE Intern:** stipend of approximately Rs. 1.0 to 1.2 lakh per month, with a pre-placement
  offer on strong performance.
