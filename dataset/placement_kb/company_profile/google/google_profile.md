# Google — Company Profile, Eligibility and Selection Process

Source: Representative sample compiled for the ResolvAI prototype. Figures are illustrative
of a typical recent cycle and are not an official Google notification. Always confirm
against the placement cell's circular for the current drive.

## About the Company
Google is an American multinational in internet search, online advertising, cloud computing
and artificial intelligence. Campus freshers are hired as **Software Engineer (SWE)**,
**STEP Intern** (for first and second year students) and **SWE Intern**.

## Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum **7.0 to 7.5 CGPA** is typical for campus shortlisting.
- **No active backlogs** at the time of the drive.
- Open primarily to CSE, IT and allied branches.
- Strong competitive programming or open-source profile improves shortlisting considerably.

## Number of Rounds
Google conducts **4 to 5 rounds**:
1. **Online assessment** — two coding problems, typically medium to hard.
2. **Technical phone screen** — one DSA problem solved in a shared editor while explaining
   the approach aloud.
3. **Onsite technical interview 1 and 2** — DSA with a strong emphasis on optimal complexity
   and clean, compiling code.
4. **Googliness and leadership round** — collaboration, dealing with ambiguity and impact.
5. **Hiring committee review** — a committee reviews the written feedback and decides; there
   is no further interview at this stage.

## Cutoff
- **No fixed percentage cutoff.** The coding rounds and the hiring committee review decide.
- Expect follow-up questions that push you to improve time and space complexity.

## CTC and Role
- **Software Engineer:** approximately Rs. 50 to 65 LPA total compensation, comprising base
  salary, a joining bonus and stock units.
- **SWE / STEP Intern:** stipend of approximately Rs. 1.2 to 1.8 lakh per month.
