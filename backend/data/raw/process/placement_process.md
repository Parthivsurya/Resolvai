# Campus Placement Process — General Guide

## Typical Stages
1. **Pre-placement talk (PPT):** the company introduces its role, tech stack, and CTC.
2. **Online assessment:** aptitude + coding, usually on a proctored platform.
3. **Technical interview:** DSA, projects, core CS subjects (OS, DBMS, CN, OOP).
4. **HR interview:** behavioural questions, culture fit, salary discussion.

## Eligibility Criteria (common)
- Minimum 60% or 6.0 CGPA throughout (10th, 12th, and current degree) is common.
- No standing arrears / active backlogs at the time of the drive for many companies.
- A gap of not more than one year in academics is often allowed.

## Cutoffs
Cutoffs vary by company. Service-based companies commonly ask for 60% aggregate, while
product-based companies may not have a strict percentage cutoff but a harder coding test.

## Off-Campus
If you miss on-campus, apply off-campus through the company careers page, referrals, and
job portals. Keep your resume and coding profiles (LeetCode, GitHub) updated.
