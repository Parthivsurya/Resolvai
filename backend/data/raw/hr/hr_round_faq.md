# HR Interview — Common Questions & Guidance

## Frequently Asked HR Questions
- Tell me about yourself.
- What are your strengths and weaknesses?
- Why do you want to join our company?
- Where do you see yourself in five years?
- Are you willing to relocate / sign a service agreement (bond)?

## Guidance
- Keep the self-introduction under a minute: education, key projects, one strength.
- Frame weaknesses with a concrete step you take to improve them.
- Research the company's products and values before the interview.
- For salary/CTC questions on freshers' roles, it is usually fixed; confirm the breakup
  (base, variable, joining bonus) politely rather than negotiating aggressively.
