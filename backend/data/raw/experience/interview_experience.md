# Interview Experiences — Sample Compilation

## Product-based company (SDE role)
Round 1 was two DSA problems (a sliding-window array question and a binary-tree traversal).
Round 2 focused on projects and system-design basics. Round 3 was a managerial + HR round
asking about teamwork and why the company. Tip: explain your thought process out loud.

## Service-based company (Systems Engineer)
The technical interview covered basics of OOP, DBMS normalization, and one simple coding
question (palindrome check). The HR round asked about relocation, bond, and strengths.

## Common advice from students
- Revise core subjects: OS, DBMS, CN, OOP — one-liners are frequently asked.
- Practice 150–200 DSA problems focused on arrays, strings, trees, and DP.
- Prepare a crisp self-introduction and two projects you can discuss in depth.
