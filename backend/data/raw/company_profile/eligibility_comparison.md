# Company-wise Eligibility, Rounds and CTC — Comparison Summary

Source: Representative sample compiled for the ResolvAI prototype, consolidating the
individual company profiles. Figures are illustrative of a typical recent cycle and are not
official notifications. Confirm against the placement cell's circular for the current drive.

## CGPA Cutoff by Company
- **TCS:** 6.0 CGPA / 60% aggregate, plus 60% in 10th and 12th.
- **Infosys:** 6.0 CGPA / 60% across 10th, 12th and degree.
- **Wipro:** 6.0 CGPA / 60% in degree, plus 60% in 10th and 12th.
- **Cognizant:** 6.0 CGPA / 60% across 10th, 12th and degree.
- **Amazon:** 6.0 CGPA minimum; coding rounds matter far more than percentage.
- **Adobe:** 7.0 CGPA typical for shortlisting.
- **Microsoft:** 7.0 CGPA typical for shortlisting.
- **Meta:** 7.0 CGPA typical for shortlisting.
- **Google:** 7.0 to 7.5 CGPA typical for shortlisting.

Service-based companies cluster at a 6.0 CGPA bar. Product-based companies shortlist at
around 7.0 CGPA and then decide almost entirely on coding performance.

## Number of Interview Rounds by Company
- **TCS:** 3 rounds — online NQT, technical interview, managerial and HR.
- **Infosys:** 3 rounds — online assessment, technical interview, HR.
- **Wipro:** 3 rounds — online assessment with essay, technical interview, HR.
- **Cognizant:** 3 rounds — online assessment, technical interview, HR.
- **Adobe:** 4 rounds — online assessment, two technical interviews, hiring manager and HR.
- **Microsoft:** 4 rounds — online assessment, two technical interviews, AA round.
- **Meta:** 4 rounds — online assessment, technical screen, two onsite rounds including
  system design and behavioural.
- **Amazon:** 4 to 5 rounds — online assessment, two or three technical interviews including
  a Bar Raiser, and a hiring manager round.
- **Google:** 4 to 5 rounds — online assessment, phone screen, two onsite technical rounds,
  Googliness round, followed by hiring committee review.

## Backlog Policy
No company in this list permits **active backlogs** at the time of the drive. TCS generally
permits at most one historical backlog. Academic gaps of 2 to 3 years are tolerated by the
service companies with documentation; product companies assess gaps case by case.
