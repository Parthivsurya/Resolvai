# Wipro — Company Profile, Eligibility and Selection Process

Source: Representative sample compiled for the ResolvAI prototype. Figures are illustrative
of a typical recent cycle and are not an official Wipro notification. Always confirm against
the placement cell's circular for the current drive.

## About the Company
Wipro Limited is an Indian multinational IT services, consulting and business process
services company. Campus freshers are hired primarily through the **Elite NTH** programme,
with a **Turbo** track for higher-performing candidates.

## Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum **60% or 6.0 CGPA** in the current degree.
- Minimum **60%** in both 10th and 12th standard.
- **No active backlogs** at the time of the drive.
- Gap in academics of **not more than 3 years**.
- Open to B.E. / B.Tech / M.E. / M.Tech / MCA across all branches.

## Number of Rounds
Wipro conducts **3 rounds**:
1. **Online assessment** — Aptitude (quantitative, logical, verbal), two coding questions,
   and a written communication (essay) test.
2. **Technical interview** — data structures, DBMS, OS, networking fundamentals and project
   discussion.
3. **HR interview** — behavioural fit, relocation, and the 12-month service agreement.

## Cutoff
- Elite NTH: clear all sections, with roughly a **60% aggregate** on the assessment.
- Turbo: a higher coding score, usually both problems solved with all test cases passing.
- The written communication test is qualifying and must be cleared.

## CTC and Role
- **Elite NTH:** approximately Rs. 3.5 LPA, role of Project Engineer.
- **Turbo:** approximately Rs. 6.5 LPA.
- A 12-month service agreement applies.
