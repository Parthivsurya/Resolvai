# TCS — Company Profile, Eligibility and Selection Process

Source: Representative sample compiled for the ResolvAI prototype. Figures are illustrative
of a typical recent cycle and are not an official TCS notification. Always confirm against
the placement cell's circular for the current drive.

## About the Company
Tata Consultancy Services is an Indian multinational IT services and consulting company and
one of the largest campus recruiters in India. Freshers are hired into three cadences:
**Ninja** (entry-level support and development), **Digital** (full-stack, cloud, analytics)
and **Prime** (advanced engineering for top coders).

## Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum **60% or 6.0 CGPA** aggregate in the current degree.
- Minimum **60%** in both 10th and 12th standard (or diploma).
- **No active backlogs** at the time of the drive. A maximum of 1 historical backlog is
  generally permitted.
- Gap in academics of **not more than 2 years**, with valid documentation.
- Open to all B.E. / B.Tech / M.E. / M.Tech / MCA / M.Sc branches.
- Age limit: candidates should be under 28 years.

## Number of Rounds
TCS conducts **3 rounds** after the online test:
1. **Online assessment (NQT)** — Numerical Ability, Verbal Ability, Reasoning Ability, and
   Programming Logic with a coding section. Roughly 180 minutes.
2. **Technical interview** — 30 to 45 minutes covering C/C++/Java fundamentals, DBMS, OS,
   and an in-depth defense of the academic capstone project.
3. **Managerial and HR interview** — 20 to 30 minutes on behavioural fit, relocation,
   service agreement and communication. Managerial may be merged with the HR round.

## Cutoff
- Ninja: roughly **60–70 percentile** on the NQT with all sections cleared.
- Digital: a significantly higher score on the advanced coding section.
- Prime: top performers from the Digital cutoff, invited by score.
- Sectional cutoffs apply — every section must be cleared individually.

## CTC and Role
- **Ninja:** approximately Rs. 3.36 LPA, role of Assistant System Engineer.
- **Digital:** approximately Rs. 7.0 LPA, role of System Engineer (Digital).
- **Prime:** approximately Rs. 9.0 to 11.5 LPA.
- Service agreement of 12 months is typical. Training at an onboarding centre precedes
  project allocation.
