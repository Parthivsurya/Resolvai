# Cognizant — Company Profile, Eligibility and Selection Process

Source: Representative sample compiled for the ResolvAI prototype. Figures are illustrative
of a typical recent cycle and are not an official Cognizant notification. Always confirm
against the placement cell's circular for the current drive.

## About the Company
Cognizant Technology Solutions is an American multinational IT services and consulting
company with a large India delivery presence. Campus hiring runs through the **GenC**
programme, with **GenC Next** and **GenC Pro** as higher tracks.

## Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum **60% or 6.0 CGPA** across 10th, 12th and the current degree.
- **No active backlogs** at the time of the drive.
- Gap in academics of **not more than 2 years**.
- Open to all B.E. / B.Tech / MCA / M.Sc branches.

## Number of Rounds
Cognizant conducts **3 rounds**:
1. **Online assessment** — aptitude, logical reasoning, verbal ability and a coding section.
2. **Technical interview** — programming fundamentals, DBMS, OOP, REST APIs and project
   discussion. GenC Next includes a harder coding discussion.
3. **HR interview** — communication, relocation and the service agreement.

## Cutoff
- GenC: clear all sections with roughly a **60% aggregate**.
- GenC Next: strong coding performance, typically both problems fully solved.
- GenC Pro: top scorers invited based on the advanced coding round.

## CTC and Role
- **GenC:** approximately Rs. 4.0 LPA, role of Programmer Analyst Trainee.
- **GenC Next:** approximately Rs. 6.75 LPA.
- **GenC Pro:** approximately Rs. 9.0 LPA.
