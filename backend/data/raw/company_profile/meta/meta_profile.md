# Meta — Company Profile, Eligibility and Selection Process

Source: Representative sample compiled for the ResolvAI prototype. Figures are illustrative
of a typical recent cycle and are not an official Meta notification. Always confirm against
the placement cell's circular for the current drive.

## About the Company
Meta Platforms builds social and messaging products at very large scale, including Facebook,
Instagram and WhatsApp, along with work in virtual and augmented reality. Campus freshers
are hired as **Software Engineer, University Grad** and as **SWE Interns**.

## Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum **7.0 CGPA** is typical for campus shortlisting.
- **No active backlogs** at the time of the drive.
- Open primarily to CSE, IT and allied branches.
- Meta recruits on a small number of campuses, so shortlisting is highly competitive.

## Number of Rounds
Meta conducts **4 rounds**:
1. **Online assessment** — two coding problems within roughly 60 minutes.
2. **Technical screen** — two DSA problems in about 45 minutes, so pace matters.
3. **Onsite coding rounds (two)** — medium-difficulty DSA problems, with speed and clean
   code valued over exotic algorithms.
4. **System design and behavioural round** — social and messaging scale problems such as
   designing the News Feed, plus questions on collaboration and impact.

## Cutoff
- **No fixed percentage cutoff.** Both problems in each coding round are generally expected
  to be solved within the time limit.

## CTC and Role
- **Software Engineer, University Grad:** approximately Rs. 50 to 60 LPA total compensation,
  comprising base salary, a joining bonus and restricted stock units.
- **SWE Intern:** stipend of approximately Rs. 1.5 lakh per month.
